## What I build

I like building for the web, making systems that can grow, and turning ideas into real products. I care a lot about keeping my code clean. I also write about tech, dev patterns, and gaming.

Day to day I work with TypeScript on Vue/Nuxt and React/Next on the frontend, and Node (Hono, Express, NestJS, Fastify) with GraphQL or REST and PostgreSQL/MySQL on the backend. I ship with Docker, Kubernetes, and Cloudflare, and test with Playwright, Cypress, and Vitest.

## Building with AI

AI coding agents are part of how I work now. I use them to plan, write, and review code, and I am comfortable owning the parts they get wrong. Most of the projects below were built this way, which let me ship in stacks I do not touch every day.
